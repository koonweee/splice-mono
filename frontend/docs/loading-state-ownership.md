## Exhaustive source-boundary audit

A TypeScript AST walk of non-test `frontend/src/**/*.tsx` (excluding generated
API code) found **41 content-boundary call sites**, **zero missing fallback props**,
and **101 direct Mantine Skeleton JSX sites** in the files listed below.
The walk checks JSX opening/self-closing elements for `DataState`,
`MobileTableList`, `DeferredFeature`, `DeferredOverlay`, `Suspense`, and
`ClientOnly`. An independent `rg` sweep included fallback props, router
pending registration, direct Skeleton usage, aliases, and `createElement` calls;
no alternate Skeleton aliases or imperative creations were found. SVG/ring
placeholders and known-control-only dialog scaffolds are covered by the companion
inventory above even though they contain no Mantine Skeleton element.

`DataState.loadingFallback`, `MobileTableList.loadingFallback`,
`DeferredFeature.fallback`, and `DeferredOverlay.skeleton` are required props.
Shared-boundary forwarding is explicit. The only four `null` content fallbacks
are the inner, ready-only Settings mobile lists: their outer DataState owns initial
loading. They never acquire a generic fallback. Source checking includes the
empty/error branch, where DataState reserves the explicitly supplied owner shape
inside an inert, hidden wrapper; it is not a separate implicit skeleton.

artwork, mutation button progress, refresh notices, notification/transaction
pagination progress, and editor submission indicators represent startup/actions,
not unknown content. They remain with their owners. Existing cached matching data
stays mounted during background refresh; failures retain retry/error semantics.

into fake financial rows, charts, or records.
